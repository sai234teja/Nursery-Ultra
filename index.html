Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our plants and services.

Do you offer delivery services?

Yes, we offer local delivery within a 25-mile radius. Delivery fees vary based on distance and order size. White-glove installation is also available for large trees and delicate rare plants.

What is your return policy for plants?

We guarantee the health of our plants for 14 days after purchase. If your plant arrives or becomes unhealthy within this time frame despite proper care, we will gladly replace it or offer a store credit.

Can I request a specific rare plant that isn't listed?

Absolutely. We have a network of specialized growers. If you are looking for a specific cultivar, please contact us and we can place a custom sourcing request for you.

Do you provide plant care instructions?

Yes! Every plant purchased from Leaf & Root comes with a detailed, printed care card tailored to that specific species, covering light, watering, and soil requirements.
